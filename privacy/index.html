QR DROP

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains how QR Drop, a plugin for Adobe Premiere, handles personal data.

The short version

QR Drop does not collect, store, send or sell any personal data. The plugin has no analytics, no tracking, no cookies and no user accounts, and it does not connect to the internet.

What happens inside the plugin

  • Everything stays on your computer. The links, Wi-Fi details, contact details, logos, colors and presets you use to make a QR code are processed locally inside Premiere. Presets and settings are saved in the plugin's own local storage on your computer. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • No internet connection. QR Drop does not request network access, so it cannot send data to any server.
  • Files and clipboard. QR Drop only reads a file (such as a logo) or writes a file (such as an exported PNG or SVG) when you choose to. It uses the clipboard only when you copy a QR code.
  • Links. If a menu item opens a web page, it opens in your own browser. That website's own privacy policy then applies.

Purchases

When you buy QR Drop through the Adobe Creative Cloud Marketplace, Adobe and its payment provider FastSpring process your order and payment details. Their own privacy policies apply. We do not receive your payment details. If we receive order details from Adobe or FastSpring, we use them only to handle your purchase, give support and meet our tax and accounting duties. Dutch law requires us to keep these records for 7 years.

When you contact us

If you email us, we use your name, email address and message only to answer you. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in giving support, or the performance of our agreement with you (GDPR Article 6(1)(b) and (f)). We delete support emails when they are no longer needed, and no later than 2 years after the case is closed, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

Children

QR Drop is a professional tool and is not aimed at children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with a new date. If a future version of QR Drop starts handling personal data, we will update this policy before that version is released.
